# No Late-Time Step in Newton's Constant from Extra Dimensions

**And the Screening Requirements of the Scale-Invariant Loophole**

Joseph Snodgrass · Independent Researcher, Los Angeles, California · zeroknot@proton.me

> **Status:** Preprint. Not yet peer reviewed. Feedback, corrections and criticism are welcome. Please open an issue or email me.

---

## Summary

The *G-step* model proposes that Newton's gravitational constant increased abruptly by about 4% at redshift z ≈ 0.01 as a way to resolve the Hubble tension. No physical mechanism for such a step is known, and extra-dimensional (Kaluza–Klein) theories are often listed as candidates.

This paper shows that extra dimensions **cannot** produce such a step, and works out what the one remaining loophole would require.

## Main results

1. **No-go result.** If G is set by the size of stabilized extra dimensions, any transition that changes G by a fraction ε shifts the vacuum energy at first order in ε:

   |ΔV| ≈ (c_K / k) · ε · m_σ² · M_P²

   Laboratory fifth-force tests require the modulus mass m_σ to be heavy, so a 4% step costs at least **10⁵⁸ times the observed dark-energy density**. The coefficient is exactly 1/3 in an explicit 6D Einstein–Maxwell flux compactification. Two independent escape routes also fail:
   - **Localized bubble:** its wall exceeds the Zel'dovich–Kobzarev–Okun bound by 31 orders of magnitude.
   - **Brane-induced Planck mass:** it requires a quartic coupling below 10⁻¹¹⁷.

2. **The only escape is a light modulus.** In a classically scale-invariant 6D bulk (gauged chiral supergravity), geometry fixes a Brans–Dicke scalar with ω = 1/2, giving a fifth force of strength **α = 1/4** relative to gravity, with no free parameter. Unscreened, this force is ruled out by Cassini, so the loophole turns the G step into a screening problem.

3. **Requirements on any screening mechanism (a "dilaton brake coupling").** Any mechanism must:
   - suppress the force in the Solar System to ≤ 4.6 × 10⁻⁵;
   - screen by environment rather than self-gravity, with a steep switch-off (index p ≳ 5);
   - leave a fraction 0.67–0.78 of Cepheid calibrators unscreened;
   - either have a range below ~0.3 Mpc or screen diffuse gas selectively.

   Known mechanisms (axio-chameleon, chameleon) fail these requirements.

## What this paper does *not* claim

- It does **not** resolve the Hubble tension. In the best allowed window H₀ ≈ 69–70 km/s/Mpc, which eases but does not remove the tension.
- It does **not** propose a working screening mechanism. It specifies what one would have to do.
- The value ω = 1/2 is a classical result and may shift once brane-localized couplings are included (see Sec. VIII of the paper).
